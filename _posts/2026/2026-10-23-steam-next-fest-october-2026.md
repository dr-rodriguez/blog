I try video game demos from time to time, and after getting back from a trip I decided to spend a couple of evenings on the October Steam Next Fest. I picked five games that were already on my radar. They were all fun, but some were definitely better than others.

---

## Emberville

[Emberville](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2295170/Emberville) has great 2D pixel art and full voice acting, and the skill and class progression looked engaging. What I didn't expect was how combat-heavy it is. I thought it promised more of a **Stardew Valley** vibe, but that wasn't in the demo.

I'm curious about the story and whether I can find a more casual experience (lowering the difficulty or trying a controller might help). Good, just not as good as I expected. Probably not a day-1 purchase, but likely eventually or on discount.

## Tropico 7

[Tropico 7](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1853440/Tropico_7) was the biggest disappointment of the bunch. It's a perfectly good Tropico game with the same music and classic humor, and it looks pretty and ran fine. The political view for edicts is a neat addition, and road management seems better. The animated character portraits feel unnecessary though, and they could just raise the system requirements.

Mostly I'm not sure what it adds over **Tropico 6**, which is still my favorite of the series. It didn't live up to the hype, so I'll watch some reviews and let's plays before deciding. I may pass, or wait for a discount.

## Trailblazers: Into the March

[Trailblazers: Into the March](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2671300/Trailblazers_Into_the_March) was very good. It feels like a mix of **RimWorld** and **FTL**, with a bit of **Against the Storm**. The core loop sounds simple, and the art is simple too, but it has a lot of charm and story-driven events.

Different factions and landships should make it replayable. The demo made me want to keep playing, which is the best thing a demo can do. This is a candidate for a day-1 purchase.

## County of Fortune

[County of Fortune](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3204330/County_of_Fortune) is a fun city/colony builder that scratched a similar itch to **Banished**. The gameplay loop is enjoyable, though it got a little repetitive, and the game felt quiet apart from the music. Some sound effects or voice acting would help it pop.

It's clever, but I couldn't tell how different it is from other games I already own. I'm holding out until I see more of it, and I'd probably grab it on a discount.

## Starfinder: Afterlight

[Starfinder: Afterlight](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3245640/Starfinder_Afterlight) was easily my favorite. It was surprisingly polished for a demo. There was some camera glitching, but the full voice acting and the art style (a mix of animated and comic-styled scenes) were great.

I couldn't tell much about the classes, maybe because some menus were disabled, but I got the gist. The little bit of story we got was engaging, and I hope the full game keeps that up. Definitely a day-1 purchase.

## Final Thoughts

**Starfinder: Afterlight** was the clear winner, and I'll be picking it up as soon as it's available. **Trailblazers: Into the March** was second: simple, but charming, with an interesting concept. **Emberville** and **County of Fortune** were both good, just not quite what I hoped for (too much combat in one, too familiar in the other), so I'll wait on those. **Tropico 7** was the letdown.
